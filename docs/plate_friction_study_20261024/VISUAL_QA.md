# Delivery visual checks

The final report was reopened with pypdf (12 pages, text on every page),
rendered with the bundled Poppler, and inspected as a full contact sheet and
at full page scale for the summary, information flow, capability comparison,
observability/outcomes, stress/2700 and numerical-audit pages. All key figures
were also inspected directly. Legends overlapping bars or margin traces were
moved into reserved space before the final build. Recorded screenshots use
the same camera and crop across conditions; no scene state was synthesized.

The PDF contains 180 dpi scientific plot rasters for a consistent reportlab
layout. The separate PDFs in figures/ retain vector lines and text for thesis
integration. Table units, moment poles, deterministic denominators, failed-run
exposure and descriptive endpoint thresholds are stated next to the results.
All pages stay within the margins and no text, chart labels or captions are
clipped. Source papers and original experimental data were not edited.
